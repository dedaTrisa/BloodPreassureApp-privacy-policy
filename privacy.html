Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

All user data (blood pressure readings, pulse values, notes, and timestamps) is stored strictly locally on your device using an internal SQLite database. No data is transmitted to external servers.

所有用户数据（血压读数、脉搏值、备注和时间戳）严格存储在您的设备本地 SQLite 数据库中。不会传输到外部服务器。

The app works entirely offline: all calculations and records remain on your device.

本应用完全离线运行：所有计算和记录均保留在您的设备上。

No data is shared with third parties.

不会与第三方共享任何数据。

Data Subject Rights / 数据主体权利

Users have full control over their data directly within the app. Measurements can be deleted manually in the History screen. Users may also delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in their device settings.

用户可以在应用中直接完全控制他们的数据。测量记录可以在“历史记录”界面手动删除。用户也可以通过卸载应用或在设备设置中清除应用数据来删除所有数据。
